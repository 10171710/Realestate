Heritage & Mission

Boutique Dedication with Modern Technology

Crestline began with two seasoned brokers and a single desk on South Congress. We expanded by doing the fundamentals right — returning calls in under 2 hours, pricing listings accurately with forensic comps, and never hiding behind call centers.

Today, we operate four offices across Central and South Texas, maintaining our strict core standard: every single client is paired with one named licensed broker who owns their transaction from initial search to key handover.

"We would rather guide 100 families through extraordinary moves than process 1,000 transactions with average service. That commitment is why over 70% of our business is repeat clients and direct referrals."

Alex Osei — Principal Broker & Managing Director (TREC #592014)
