Common Questions

Connecting With Our Team

How fast can an agent arrange a private home tour?

For active MLS listings, we can typically schedule same-day or next-day in-person viewings. If you are relocating from another city, our agents can conduct 4K live video walk-throughs on demand with real-time Q&A.

Are weekend and evening showings available?

Yes. All Crestline buyer specialists accommodate Saturday and Sunday private showings as well as weekday evening tours up to 7:30pm CT.

How do I submit an emergency maintenance request for a rental property?

Current tenants can submit emergency work orders directly through their Customer Dashboard or by calling our 24/7 emergency dispatch line at +1 (512) 555-0199.
