FAQ

Quick Answers

Are the listings on this page updated in real time?

Yes. Availability, pricing and open-house times sync from the MLS every few minutes. If a home is under contract, it leaves the grid automatically.

Can I make an offer straight from a listing?

Once you're signed into the customer dashboard, yes — your agent prepares the offer with you, submits it, and every step of the negotiation appears in the tracker.

Is my saved-shortlist private?

Completely. Your saved homes are visible only to you and your assigned agent, and never shared with sellers or third parties.
