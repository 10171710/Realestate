Consumer Protection FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything buyers, sellers and landlords need to know about our brokerage standards.

How does Crestline charge $0 in buyer agent fees?

Buyer representation through Crestline Realty Group is 100% free to the buyer. Under standard Texas real estate practice, buyer agent compensation is covered directly by the seller or listing broker at closing. You receive full fiduciary representation, private showings, and contract negotiation at zero out-of-pocket cost.

What is included in the $299 Flat-Fee MLS vs. 2.5% Full-Service Seller Plan?

Our $299 Flat-Fee plan gives self-directed sellers MLS syndication to Zillow, Realtor.com, and Redfin while managing their own showings. Our 2.5% Full-Service plan provides complete broker management: professional HDR photography, 3D virtual tour, open house hosting, counter-offer negotiations, and closing coordination.

Can I schedule private in-person or live video property tours online?

Yes. Open any listing on our portal and select your preferred date, time, and format (In-Person or Live Video Walkthrough). Your dedicated licensed agent coordinates access with the seller and confirms your slot instantly in your client dashboard.

Are all Crestline agents licensed by the Texas Real Estate Commission?

Yes. Crestline Realty Group operates under Texas Real Estate Commission (TREC) Broker License #9004812. Every broker and agent on our team holds an active license, carries full errors & omissions insurance, and adheres to strict National Association of REALTORS® ethical standards.
