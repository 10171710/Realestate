Consumer Protection

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding representation, commissions, and legal disclosures.

Do buyers pay Crestline any commissions directly?

Under Texas real estate guidelines and MLS agreements, buyer agent compensation is traditionally paid out of the seller's proceeds at closing. Browsing properties, scheduling private showings, and preparing offers through Crestline is completely free to buyers.

How does the $299 flat-fee listing differ from the 2.5% full-service plan?

The $299 flat-fee option gives you MLS syndication across Zillow, Realtor.com, and Redfin for 60 days where you manage viewings yourself. The 2.5% full-service plan includes a dedicated listing agent, staging consultation, professional HDR media, physical open houses, and full contract negotiation through closing.

What guarantees do you offer for landlords and property management?

We offer a 30-Day Tenant Placement Guarantee and an Eviction Protection Shield. If a tenant placed by Crestline defaults within the first 6 months, we find a qualified replacement tenant at zero additional leasing fee.

Can I cancel a listing agreement if I change my mind?

Yes. We offer an unconditional satisfaction guarantee. If you are ever unhappy with our marketing performance or agent representation before accepting an offer, you may terminate your agreement with zero penalty fees.
