Pricing FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do buyers ever pay Crestline directly?

In almost every transaction, no. Buyer broker commission is paid out of the seller's proceeds at closing, so browsing, touring, and making offers through Crestline is completely free to you.

Can I cancel or switch a seller listing plan?

Yes. We offer an unconditional satisfaction guarantee. You may cancel your listing agreement at any time before accepting an offer with zero penalty fees.

Are there fees for scheduling viewings or messaging agents?

No. Scheduling in-person or live video tours, messaging your assigned agent, and tracking offers inside your customer dashboard are included with zero extra charge.
