Property Overview

A meticulously maintained contemporary residence situated on a quiet cul-de-sac in the coveted Alder Ridge enclave. The expansive ground floor showcases an open architectural layout uniting a chef-grade quartz island kitchen, custom pantry, dining suite, and great room featuring oversized European glass sliders opening to a covered patio and fully fenced private garden.

The upper level comprises four generous bedrooms, highlighted by a primary suite sanctuary with custom dual walk-in closets, frameless rainfall glass shower, and freestanding soaking tub. Fitted with owned 6.2kW rooftop solar panels, dual-zone smart thermostats, and a tankless water heater, providing exceptional energy efficiency.
